Proposal v1 for mobve.com and the staff app

Mobve Design System

One set of values for the public website and the staff workspace, rebuilt from a small base: a primary, a secondary, an ink, a paper and two grays. Everything else is calculated.

Inter Variable Primary #0b63ceSecondary #ffc845 8 px, 14 px, pill 4 px spacing base 44 px touch targets

What this fixes

One scaleThe website uses 20 font sizes and 8 weights. The system keeps 9 text roles and 3 weights.
Six base colorsThe theme had 30 color variables and six blues. It now has a primary, a secondary, an ink, a paper, a text gray, a background gray and three semantic signals. States and tints are calculated.
Every state definedHover, pressed, focus, disabled, loading and error exist for every control, with exact values.
One font on both productsThe site names Inter but never loads it. The app loads Inter Variable. Both load it from their own server.
Readable sizesNothing smaller than 12 px. Body text is 16 px on the site. Controls are 44 px high.
Measured, not guessedCurrent values come from computed styles on the live pages. Every contrast ratio below is calculated.

All specimens on this page use the system's own tokens and classes. Hover and keyboard focus work on every live example. The Inter font loads from Google Fonts here only to render the specimens; on mobve.com it must be self-hosted because the site's security policy allows only its own files.

Color

Route A. One blue does every "act here" job, including the logo and information messages. A warm yellow carries highlights, featured items and the second chart series; it is never text on paper. One gray serves secondary text and control borders, and #fafafa, the light gray the app already uses, is the page background under white cards and panels. The blue tint is kept for interactive states. Status colors follow traffic lights: green, amber and red, each with a dark text in the same hue and a light tint for backgrounds.

Each signal keeps the hue of its traffic light (green 150, amber 72, red 27 in OKLCH) at a similar lightness, so the three read as a set. The signal marks the state: badge dot, notice border, error border, destructive button, chart status. Text stays in the dark version, which reaches 6 to 7.5:1 on its tint. The real traffic amber (#ffbf00) is almost the same as the secondary yellow, so the warning moves toward orange (#f7a30d); keep the secondary away from status and always show a warning with its icon and text.

Appointment categories, 5

The app colors appointments by purpose. The original five colors collided with the new palette (Tour with the primary, Maintenance with error, Billing between blue and violet). Tour and Billing sit at the lightness of the success signal; Lease, Inspection and Maintenance are deeper. Their hues avoid the primary, the secondary and the orange-red arc used by warning and error, and are at least 0.12 apart in OKLab from each other and from every brand and semantic color. They mark dots, event borders and bars. Text on them is always ink on a 12 percent tint, never the category color on paper.

Typography

Inter Variable, three weights (400, 500, 600). Headings step down fluidly on small screens. Text blocks stop at 65 characters. Headline tracking is -0.02em, which is safe with Inter and avoids the risk of touching letters that -0.055em brings when the font falls back to the system face.

Components

Every component shows its states with forced classes (.is-hover, .is-active, .is-focus) so they can be compared side by side. The real :hover, :active and :focus-visible selectors work as well.

Button

DefaultHoverPressedFocusDisabledLoading Primary Secondary Ghost Danger

Height 44 px, padding 24 px, radius 8 px, label 15 px weight 600. Hover: 22 percent darker, lifts 1 px, shadow level 2. Pressed: 44 percent darker, sinks 1 px, inner shadow. Each step changes color and position, so the three states are distinct. Disabled is 38 percent opacity with the label still readable. Compact (36 px) is allowed only for dense tables used with a mouse.

Using it

  1. Self-host InterVariable.woff2 and enable the commented @font-face at the top of the tokens. The site's policy (default-src 'self') blocks Google Fonts.
  2. Load the tokens first, then base and components, then page styles. In the app, review the --primary: #0a88db token (3.8:1 with white) and align it to the primary.
  3. Use token names in new CSS and replace the values listed in the migration map as pages are touched.

Open decisions

  • Dark mode is not proposed. Both products are light, and the demo section already carries a dark band.
  • Icons are limited to the four status icons shown. An icon set for the app needs its own decision.
  • Charts need their own palette. Use the primary and the secondary as the first two series until a data palette is designed.
  • Logo color: route A moves the logo blue from #0878f9 to #0b63ce so that one blue passes contrast everywhere. This is a brand decision for the client; the logo files are not changed here.
  • Calculated colors use color-mix(), supported by all current browsers. If an older browser must be supported, replace the calculated tokens with their results from the table above.
  • Recommended plan in the pricing pattern is an example. The owner chooses.
  • To validate on the real pages: check headline tracking and line breaks with Inter loaded, and test the type scale on a phone.
